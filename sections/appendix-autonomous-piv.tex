\section{High-Speed Autonomous PIV Data Templates (Informative)}

This appendix records the PIV Auto challenge, ISO/IEC~7816
\texttt{GENERAL AUTHENTICATE} templates, and fixture sources used by the
demonstration vectors. The APDU examples are validated by the repository tests
against the same helper code used by the simulator and replay tools.

\subsection{Fixed-Width KMAC Challenge Template}
\begin{SimpleTable}{PIV Auto KMAC Challenge Template}{4}
  {|L{0.23\SimpleUsableWidth}|L{0.10\SimpleUsableWidth}|L{0.21\SimpleUsableWidth}|L{0.46\SimpleUsableWidth}|}
  {\TableHeaderCell{Field} & \TableHeaderCell{Size} & \TableHeaderCell{Encoding} & \TableHeaderCell{Notes}}
  Template label & 14 & ASCII plus version byte & \code{OSDP-PIV-AUTO} followed by \ApduChallenge{01}. \\\hline
  Series Counter & 4 & LSB first & ACU-managed boot or epoch counter, for example \ApduChallenge{07000000}. \\\hline
  Sequence Counter & 4 & LSB first & ACU-managed per-series counter, for example \ApduChallenge{2A000000}. \\\hline
  FASC-N & 25 & Raw bytes & Credential FASC-N from CHUID. \\\hline
  UUID & 16 & Raw bytes & Credential UUID. Successful PIV Auto examples use the credential UUID; unavailable UUID is a failure condition. \\\hline
  PIV Key Reference & 1 & Raw byte & Challenged key reference, normally \ApduHeader{9A} or \ApduHeader{9E}. \\\hline
  PIV Algorithm & 1 & Raw byte & Algorithm identifier: \ApduHeader{05}, \ApduHeader{06}, \ApduHeader{07}, \ApduHeader{11}, or \ApduHeader{14}. \\\hline
  Supplemental Entropy & 32 & Raw bytes & Fixed-width ACU entropy field; all zeroes are valid for test vectors only. \\\hline
\end{SimpleTable}

The challenge digest is produced in two steps. First, derive
\code{PIV-AUTO-KDK = KMAC256(key = S-ENC || S-MAC1 || S-MAC2, data = empty, mac\_len = 32, custom = "OSDP-PIV-AUTO-KDK-v1")}.
Second, derive the challenge as
\code{KMAC256(key = PIV-AUTO-KDK, data = fixed-width template, mac\_len = L, custom = "OSDP-PIV-AUTO-CHALLENGE-v1")}.
\code{L} is 32 bytes for SHA-256 profiles and 48 bytes for SHA-384 profiles.
The supplemental entropy field is always present and fixed at 32 bytes; all
zeroes are valid for test vectors but not recommended for deployments.
